## AI-Powered Luggage Transportation Management and Tracking System

**Tagline:** *Your Luggage Moves. You Move Freely.*

LUGGO AI is a smart luggage transportation management and tracking system designed for railway stations and bus terminals. It helps passengers transfer luggage between different platforms or bus bays when they have limited time or difficulty carrying heavy luggage.

## 🚨 Problem
"The main problem is that passengers in railway stations and bus terminals often struggle to carry heavy luggage from one platform or bus bay to another. This is especially difficult when the station is crowded or when the passenger has less time to reach their train or bus".
## 💡 Solution
To solve this, we introduced LUGGO AI. Through our system, passengers can book luggage transportation by selecting the source and destination. The luggage is then physically moved by our staff, while the system provides QR-based verification and tracking. Once the luggage reaches the destination, the passenger verifies it with an OTP and collects it safely.

So, our main idea is simple: the passenger moves freely, while LUGGO takes care of the luggage.”

## ✨ Key Features

- 🚆 Railway platform-to-platform luggage transfer
- 🚌 Bus terminal/bay-to-bay luggage transfer
- 🤖 AI-based priority management
- 👤 Authorized human-assisted luggage transportation
- 📱 Digital luggage booking
- 🔐 QR and OTP-based verification
- 📍 Luggage transportation status tracking
- 📋 Passenger-specific booking history
- ♿ Assisted booking for users with different levels of digital literacy

## 🔄 Workflow

**Book → Priority Assignment → Luggage Handover → Pickup → In Transit → Destination Reached → QR/OTP Verification → Passenger Collects Luggage**

## 🤖 Role of AI

The AI-based priority engine considers factors such as:

- Time remaining before train/bus departure
- Distance between source and destination
- Pending luggage requests
- Availability of authorized handlers
- Passenger urgency

This helps prioritize urgent luggage-transfer requests.

## 🔐 Security

LUGGO uses:

- Unique Booking ID
- QR Code
- OTP Verification
- Digital status tracking

These provide better traceability and secure luggage handover.
